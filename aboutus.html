OUR STORY

From a physical game to a digital product

Trio was created with the belief that mathematical thinking can be competitive, social and genuinely enjoyable.

Making mathematics part of the game

Instead of treating maths as a separate learning activity, Trio places calculation, observation and decision-making at the centre of the gameplay.

The beginning of Trio

Trio's development began in 2021 after Hasan Aydinoglu learned the basic version of the game from Stephen Milton during a visit to the United Kingdom. Stephen had previously developed the basic version and introduced Hasan to its original concept.

With Stephen's permission, Hasan took the project forward and adapted the basic game into a product for Turkey. He then created the advanced versions independently, adding new ways to play while preserving Trio's mathematical and strategic foundations.

Hasan then took responsibility for turning the expanded game into a commercial physical product, working through product development, production, sales, customer communication and fulfilment as part of an independent games business.

The project later expanded beyond the physical game. Hasan Aydinoglu independently developed Trio's digital direction, building the mobile application and extending the product with advanced game modes and connected features.

Today, Trio represents the connection between a commercially tested physical product and an evolving digital platform designed for wider accessibility and international growth.

THE FOUNDER

Building across product and technology

Hasan Aydinoglu took Trio forward after learning its basic version from Stephen Milton in the United Kingdom. With Stephen's permission, Hasan adapted the concept, created the advanced versions independently and led its product, commercial and digital development.

From 2021 onward, Hasan developed Trio from that basic starting point into a broader product, later building the mobile application using modern digital technologies.

The mobile product has been developed to support multiple game modes, user registration, profiles, friends, messaging and multiplayer-oriented gameplay.

This work reflects the continuous development of Trio from a standalone physical game into a connected digital product.

Hasan is also a co-founder of the UK-based company Narrative Circle Limited, supporting the longer-term objective of developing original products and building an international presence in the digital games sector.
